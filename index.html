01 — About

A foundation in
planning and place.

Dr. Navneet Munoth is an architect and urban planner, serving as an Associate Professor in the Department of Architecture and Planning at the Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology (MANIT), Bhopal. He has been part of MANIT’s academic community since 2010.

He completed his Bachelor of Architecture at the National Institute of Technology, Hamirpur, and earned both his Master of Planning and PhD in Urban Planning and Management from the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee. His teaching and research engage with the evolving challenges of cities and the built environment.

Alongside his academic work, he serves as Honorary Director of the Council of Architecture’s Training and Research Centre at MANIT Bhopal, supporting learning and professional development in architecture and planning.
